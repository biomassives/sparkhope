# Nutrient Solution Preparation for Lion's Mane (*Hericium erinaceus*)

This guide provides a simple and effective method for preparing a nutrient solution for growing Lion's Mane in a sterile loop liquid culture system. The focus is on low-resource techniques suitable for amateur mycologists.

## Ideal Nutrient Composition
The nutrient solution should provide essential carbohydrates, nitrogen, vitamins, and minerals to support mycelial growth. Here are the key components:

1. **Light Malt Extract**: A primary source of carbohydrates.
2. **Dextrose**: A simple sugar that serves as an energy source.
3. **Peptone**: Optional, but it provides amino acids that enhance growth.
4. **Yeast Extract**: Optional, rich in vitamins and minerals.

## Nutrient Solution Recipe
For preparing the nutrient solution, use the following recipe:

- **For 1 Liter of Liquid Culture**:
  - 10 g Light Malt Extract
  - 5 g Dextrose
  - 1-2 g Peptone (optional)
  - 1 g Yeast Extract (optional)
  - Adjust the pH to 5.5-6.5 before sterilization.

## Preparation Steps
1. **Mix Ingredients**: In a clean container, combine the light malt extract, dextrose, peptone, and yeast extract with distilled water.
2. **Adjust pH**: Use pH test strips (available at garden or aquarium stores) to check the pH. Adjust if necessary using vinegar (to lower pH) or baking soda (to raise pH).
3. **Sterilization**: Pour the nutrient solution into clean glass jars or bottles. Sterilize using a pressure cooker at 15 psi for 30-60 minutes. If you don’t have a pressure cooker, boiling for 30 minutes can be a simpler alternative, though it may not be as effective.
4. **Cooling**: Allow the sterilized nutrient solution to cool in a clean area before inoculating with mycelium.
