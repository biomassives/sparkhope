## Oyster Mushroom Mycelial Cake and Aerial Mycelium

In two labs, we are working to demonstrate a side by side (TOL lab equipment based culturing and DIY waste stream derived improvisation setups) to demonstrate that mycelia can be selected from the wild, expanded in agar and liquid culture solution, and grown myelial mass ( or mats - in the case of aerial mycelium ) and harvested for use as a viable food source for human consumotion.

### Techniques

- Aquision of culture from wild sources
- Expansion of mycelia in agar, liquid culture, and on grain
- Decontruction and substitution of ingredients 
- Careful management of lab sterility in improvised conditions

## Management of cuture and liquid culture loop

Lab use of Pleurotis Otreatis, Pleurotis Puminarius , Herecium Eneracea, and Inonotus Obliquus ( oyster, high protein oyster, lions mane, and Chaga respectively)
