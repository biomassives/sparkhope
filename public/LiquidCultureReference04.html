🔬 Educational Prep Flow: Maximum Bioavailability

This flow explains how to use ingredients like Pasta Water (high protein/starch) for optimal results by performing a simple Nutrient Tea Boil (NTB) and Enzymatic Pre-treatment before final sterilization.

DAY 1: Extract & Pre-Digestion
  • Extraction: Boil or soak your Protein Builder (Bean/Pasta Water) or Energy Source (Malted Grain).
  • Yeast Boost (Optional): Add a pinch of dried yeast to the liquid. The yeast enzymes immediately start breaking down complex starches and proteins into simple sugars and amino acids.
  • Overnight Rest: Allow the mixture to rest at room temp for 8-12 hours (The Pre-Digestion phase).
↓
DAY 2: Assembly & Sterilization
  • Assembly: Combine your Filtered Water Base (90%) with the Pre-Digested Liquid (10%). Add Structure/Vitamins (e.g., Eggshell Powder).
  • Jars: Pour into LC jars with filters/ports.
  • Sterilization: Pressure cook the final, assembled solution at 15 PSI for 30 minutes. This kills the pre-digestion yeast, leaving a sterile, nutrient-maximized broth.
