🍄 Myco Lab Substrate Reference

Selection, Preparation & Supporting Resources

Substrate Ingredients

  • 🌾 Grains – Rye, millet, sorghum
  • 🍂 Lignocellulosic – Sawdust, cardboard, straw, coffee grounds
  • 🥚 Supplements – Gypsum, bran, eggshell powder
  • 🐛 Alternative nitrogen sources – Legume soak water, insect meal

Preparation Steps

  • Soak grains for 12–24h (hydrate & reduce bacterial load)
  • Boil/simmer grains until 70–80% hydrated
  • Drain & surface-dry until no standing water remains
  • Mix with supplements (gypsum, bran, Ca sources)
  • Load into jars/bags & sterilize (pressure cooker/autoclave)
  • Cool before inoculation
