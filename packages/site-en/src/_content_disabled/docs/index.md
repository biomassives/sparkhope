## MycoLab Database

Welcome to MycoLab - your complete mushroom cultivation database with 12 species, 25 ingredients, 17 recipes, 9 batches, and 15 observations.

### Browse Your Data

- **[Species](/species/)** - Browse all 12 mushroom species with cultivation parameters
- **[Ingredients](/ingredients/)** - View all 25 ingredients used in recipes  
- **[Recipes](/recipes/)** - Explore 17 cultivation recipes and formulations
- **[Batches](/batches/)** - Track all 9 active and completed cultivation batches
- **[Observations](/observations/)** - Review 15 detailed grow logs and observations

### Features

- 📊 **Data-Driven** - All data from your SQLite database
- 🔄 **Dynamic Pages** - Automatically generated from JSON
- 🎨 **Beautiful UI** - Clean, modern interface with Starlight
- 🔗 **Linked Data** - Cross-references between batches, recipes, and observations

### Your Database

Your MycoLab database includes:
- Blue Oyster, Lions Mane, Chaga, Shiitake, and more
- Liquid culture, agar, and grain spawn recipes
- Active batches with full observation tracking
- Complete ingredient library with preparation notes

Start exploring by clicking any section in the sidebar!
