### Rationale for Data Population

1.  **Sugar Tolerance (`lc_sugar_tolerance_max`):**

      * *Pleurotus* species (7, 10, 13) are assigned a **6%** maximum tolerance, as research indicates they can thrive in higher nutrient concentrations compared to standard $4\%$ solutions.[1]
      * *Hericium* and *Shiitake* (8, 9, 11, 12) are assigned a **$4\%$** maximum, as $4\%$ is the generally recommended optimal concentration to prevent caramelization during sterilization.[2]

2.  **Aseptic Requirement (`aseptic_requirement`):**

      * Robust secondary decomposers like Oyster mushrooms (7, 10) are set to **Moderate (SAB Viable)**, reflecting their competitive nature and suitability for low-cost setups.[3, 1]
      * Species requiring highly supplemented or sterilized media (*Shiitake*, *King Oyster*) or those specifically grown for delicate culture work (*Lion's Mane*, *Chaga*) are designated **High**, necessitating Laminar Flow Hood (LFH) or rigorous pressure sterilization for consistent success.[3, 4]

3.  **Low-Resource Disinfection (`low_resource_disinfection`):**

      * Oyster species (7, 10) are ideal candidates for **Cold Lime Bath** or **Solarization**, which are effective low-energy pasteurization methods for straw.[5, 6]
      * The species requiring supplemented sawdust (8, 11, 12, 13) must use **Autoclave/Pressure Cooker** as pasteurization is insufficient for nutrient-rich substrates.[7]

4.  **Tissue Cloning Size (`tissue_cloning_size`):**

      * *P. ostreatus* (Oyster) and *P. eryngii* (King Oyster) are set to **Large Plug**, as larger tissue sizes have been shown to produce faster mycelial growth and higher density.[8]
      * *Lentinula edodes* (Shiitake) is set to **Small Plug**, as small plugs were observed to favor mycelial growth for this species.[8]

5.  **Food Security Focus (`food_security_focus`):**

      * *King Oyster* (13) and *Lion's Mane* (8, 12) are prioritized for **Biomass (SmF)** because research highlights their exceptional nutritional density and high protein yield when produced via submerged liquid culture.[9, 10]
